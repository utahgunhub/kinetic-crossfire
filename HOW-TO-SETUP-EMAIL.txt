================================================================================
HOW TO SETUP YOUR EMAILJS TEMPLATE - STEP BY STEP
================================================================================

1. Go to: https://dashboard.emailjs.com/admin/templates/template_xba6pcp

2. In the EmailJS template editor:
   
   A. SUBJECT LINE:
      Order Confirmation - {{order_number}} - Kinetic Crossfire
   
   B. CONTENT (Switch to HTML editor):
      - Open the file: emailjs-template.html
      - Copy ALL the contents
      - Paste into the HTML editor in EmailJS
   
   C. SETTINGS:
      - To Email: {{to_email}}
      - From Name: Kinetic Crossfire
      - Reply To: Your support email address

3. Click "Save"

4. Test it by placing an order on your website!

================================================================================
